# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 28, 2026

## About Bag Count

Bag Count is a simple scorekeeper for cornhole and other two-team bag games. No account is required to use the app.

## Data We Collect

**Bag Count does not collect personal data.**

We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, location, or other personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

## Data Stored on Your Device

When you save a finished game, Bag Count stores that game locally on your device (scores, team colors, winner, date, and optional timer duration). This data:

- stays on your device
- is not uploaded to our servers
- is not shared with third parties
- can be cleared at any time from the History screen

If you delete the app, this local data is removed with it.

## Children

Bag Count is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
